Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What happens during a comprehensive eye exam?
A comprehensive eye exam at EYEDEAL Eyecare covers a vision and refraction check, eye pressure testing, and a detailed look at the retina and overall eye health. It's designed to catch both vision changes and early signs of eye disease.
At what age should my child have their first eye exam?
Most children benefit from their first pediatric eye exam before starting school, though we welcome younger children too. Early exams help catch vision issues that can affect learning before they become harder to correct.
How do I know if I have dry eye, and can it be treated?
Symptoms like grittiness, burning, redness, or blurry vision that comes and goes are common signs of dry eye. During your visit, we identify the underlying cause and build a dry eye treatment plan suited to your specific symptoms.
What is myopia management, and is it right for my child?
Myopia management uses proven methods to help slow the progression of nearsightedness in children and teens. If your child's prescription keeps increasing, it's worth discussing myopia management options at their next exam.
Do I need an appointment, or do you accept walk-ins?
We recommend scheduling ahead so we can give your comprehensive eye exam or consultation the full time it deserves. Call us at 973-604-2020 and our team will find a time that works for you.
